Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Tuku (টুকু) is a baby care app for parents. It helps you keep track of vaccines, first foods, allergens and milestones. This page explains what the app saves, where it's kept, and how you can delete it. It covers the Tuku mobile app only.

What Tuku saves

Your child's details. The name, birth date and sex you enter, and the picture you pick for your child in the app.

Vaccines. Which doses your child has had, the dates they were given, your choice on optional vaccines, and any notes you write.

Food and allergens. The foods your child has tried, when and how often, any reaction you noted, and your notes. For allergen watches, we save the allergen, the day you started it, the day the watch ends, and how it went.

Milestones. Your answers to the milestone questions, and the date your child learned each one.

Your settings. Which reminders are on, the time of day you picked, which child is selected, and your text size.

Where your information is kept

Everything stays on your phone, in a small database inside the app. Tuku has no server and no account. You never sign in. The app doesn't send your information anywhere, and we can't see it.

Android backup. Android can back up app data to your own Google account. If you have phone backup switched on, Android may include Tuku's data in that backup, so you can get it back on a new phone. This backup belongs to you and is handled by Google, not by us. If you don't want this, turn off backup in your phone's Settings, under Google or System, then Backup.

What Tuku doesn't collect

  • No analytics or tracking. There's no analytics code in the app, no advertising ID, and no third party watching what you do.
  • No crash reports. The app doesn't send any error or usage reports.
  • No ads. We don't show ads, and we never sell or share your information.
  • No location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. Tuku never asks for any of them.
  • No push notification tokens. Your reminders are scheduled on your own phone, so nothing about them is sent to a server.

The only permission Tuku asks for is to show notifications, so it can remind you about vaccines, daily foods and milestones. You can say no, or switch reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can do that whenever you like:

  • Remove a child by opening that child's details in the app and choosing to delete their information. That clears their vaccine card, food list, milestones and reminders.
  • Remove everything by clearing Tuku's data in your phone's app settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Deleting happens straight away, and it can't be undone. There is no copy on any server for us to delete. If Android backup is on, an older copy may still be in your Google account backup until Google replaces or removes it.

Children

Tuku is made for parents and caregivers, not for children. It saves information about a child that a parent enters, and that information never leaves the phone.

Health information

Tuku gives information and reminders. It isn't medical advice. Always follow your child's doctor and the national EPI vaccine schedule.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version goes on this page and the date at the top changes with it.
