What Nurture does

Nurture reminds you when your plants might need watering. It works out the timing from general guidance for the species you pick and the dates you record.

It's a reminder tool, not gardening advice. Watering intervals are rough estimates. Real plants depend on light, humidity, pot size, soil and the season, and the app can't measure any of that. Trust your own judgement and check the soil before you water. We can't promise a plant will thrive, and we're not responsible if one doesn't.
